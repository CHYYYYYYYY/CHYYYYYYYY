# Hi, I'm Haoyu Cheng 👋

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering master's student at **UC San Diego**, working on multimodal reasoning, neuro-symbolic AI, and physical world models. I previously completed my B.S. in Computer Science at the **University of Liverpool**, graduating with a 3.90/4.0 GPA and ranking 3rd among 286 students.

My research focuses on building AI systems that can understand diagrams and physical scenes, reason with structured knowledge, and produce verifiable solutions.

## 🔬 Current focus

- Multimodal large language models and visual reasoning
- Neuro-symbolic methods for geometry and physics
- Physical video encoders and world-model learning
- Reliable benchmarks, data pipelines, and evaluation

## 📌 Selected work

- **Hilbert-Geo** — a neural-symbolic framework for solving solid geometry problems, published at **CVPR 2026**. [Project repository](https://github.com/CHYYYYYYYY/SolidGeoSolver)
- **Omni-Geo** — a full-domain multimodal geometry benchmark covering plane, analytic, and solid geometry. [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/omnigeo/OmniGeo)
- **PhysElite** — a bilingual multimodal benchmark for Olympiad-level physics reasoning. [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/physelite/PhysElite)
- **ICBC DataFlow** — an operations-agent pipeline for alert analysis, fault localization, incident response, and data generation. [Project repository](https://github.com/CHYYYYYYYY/icbc-dataflow)
